If you are writing the PDE in February, the dates that matter are:

- Registration and payment are open from 4 to 29 January 2027, through the MyPPRA portal only
- Pay with your correct 7-digit reference number and keep your proof of payment
- The exam on 11 February is a take-home exam unless the PPRA says otherwise
- Postponement requests must reach the PPRA at least 14 days before the exam

After the portal problems at the 2026 sittings, we suggest registering early and checking that your seat number appears on the portal.
