If you are writing the PDE this year, the dates that matter are:

- Registration and payment close on 30 October, through the MyPPRA portal only
- Pay with your correct 7-digit reference number and keep your proof of payment
- The exam on 12 November is a take-home exam unless the PPRA says otherwise
- Postponement requests must reach the PPRA at least 14 days before the exam

After the portal problems at the February and May sittings, we suggest registering early and checking that your seat number appears on the portal.
